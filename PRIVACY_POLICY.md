# Privacy Policy

## Overview

This privacy policy explains how Bowling HQ collects, uses, and protects your data. As a personal project, privacy is treated with utmost care.

## Data Collection

Bowling HQ collects the following types of data:

### User Data
- Username and email
- Profile information
- Preferences and settings
- Authentication credentials (securely hashed)

### Bowling Performance Data
- Game scores
- Frame-by-frame results
- Equipment used
- Lane information
- Venue details
- Session date/time

### Video Data (Optional)
- Bowling videos you upload
- Video metadata
- Analysis results from videos

### Analytics Data
- How you use the platform
- Features accessed
- Time spent
- Device information
- Browser information

## Data Usage

Your data is used to:

1. **Provide Services**
   - Track your performance
   - Generate analysis and insights
   - Provide AI coaching recommendations
   - Compare performance metrics

2. **Improve Platform**
   - Understand feature usage
   - Identify improvements needed
   - Optimize performance
   - Fix bugs

3. **Personalization**
   - Customize your experience
   - Show relevant recommendations
   - Tailor learning paths
   - Suggest improvements

4. **Security & Safety**
   - Prevent fraud
   - Protect account security
   - Comply with legal requirements

## Data Sharing

**Your data is never shared with third parties except:**

- **Service Providers**: Cloud hosting, analytics (with privacy agreements)
- **Legal Requirements**: If required by law enforcement
- **Your Choice**: When you explicitly share with coaches or friends

## Video Privacy

Bowling videos are treated with special care:

- Stored encrypted
- Private by default
- Only you can view unless you share
- Can be deleted anytime
- Not used for training AI without consent

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: Data kept as long as account is active
- **Deleted Accounts**: Most data deleted within 30 days
- **Backups**: May retain backups for up to 90 days
- **Videos**: Deleted immediately when you request
- **Analytics**: Anonymized after 1 year

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

1. **Access** - Request all data we have about you
2. **Correct** - Fix inaccurate information
3. **Delete** - Remove your account and data
4. **Export** - Get your data in portable format
5. **Opt-out** - Decline analytics tracking
6. **Withdraw Consent** - Revoke any permissions given

## Security

We protect your data with:

- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- **Hashing**: Passwords hashed with bcrypt/argon2
- **Access Control**: Role-based permissions
- **Auditing**: Regular security audits
- **Updates**: Regular security patches

## Compliance

- GDPR (EU): Yes, we comply
- CCPA (California): Yes, we comply
- Children's Privacy: We don't knowingly collect data from users under 13

## Cookie Policy

We use cookies for:
- Session management (authentication)
- Preferences (UI settings)
- Analytics (optional, can be disabled)

Cookies are:
- Secure (HTTPS only)
- HttpOnly (no JavaScript access)
- SameSite (CSRF protection)

## Do Not Track

If you enable "Do Not Track" in your browser, we respect that setting and won't track analytics.

## Data Breach

If a data breach occurs:
- Users will be notified within 48 hours
- Details of what data was accessed
- Steps we're taking to remediate
- Recommended actions for users
